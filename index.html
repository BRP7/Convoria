What we automate

We do not automate everything. We automate repeatable work that leaks time, follow-ups, or visibility.

The best projects have volume, clear rules, and a painful handoff between people or tools.

01

Lead intake & qualification

Capture leads from WhatsApp, forms, email, or sheets. Ask basic questions, tag quality, assign owners, and create reminders.

02

Email-to-ticket

Classify incoming emails, extract key fields, create tickets/tasks, and route unclear cases to manual review.

03

CRM/ERP updates

Reduce copy-paste between messages, sheets, CRM, ERP, and internal tools.

04

Follow-up reminders

Trigger reminders for sales, support, collections, approvals, or pending actions.

05

Reports & visibility

Show open tasks, missed actions, pending follow-ups, and completed items without manual reporting.

06

Manual review queues

When automation is unsure, route the item to a human instead of silently making bad decisions.

Fit filter

This is not for every business.

Good fit

  • You receive repeated leads, requests, tickets, or follow-ups
  • Your team uses WhatsApp, email, CRM, ERP, or sheets
  • Manual tracking is causing missed work or poor visibility
  • You can provide access and make decisions quickly
  • Budget starts from ₹25K for simple pilots

Not a fit

  • You want a ₹5K full custom automation
  • You are only exploring AI with no business workflow
  • You cannot provide tool access or sample data
  • Your process changes every day and has no repeatable pattern
  • You want full custom software at automation pricing

Payment terms

  • Small projects: 70% upfront, 30% before handover
  • Medium projects: 50% upfront, 30% after working demo, 20% before go-live
  • Monthly support is paid in advance
  • New workflows or major changes are quoted separately
