The manual email-to-ticket process

From inbox handling to a logged, assigned ticket.

When each request is processed by hand, small delays and missed details add up. The workflow can handle the repeatable steps and leave exceptions for human review.

Before Convoria

  • Someone reads each email and works out who the customer is
  • The issue and urgency are identified manually
  • Details are copied into a ticketing or CRM system
  • A ticket is created and assigned by hand
  • Confirmation and status logging can be missed

After Convoria

  • Incoming email is captured and classified
  • Customer, issue, and required fields are extracted
  • Ticket is created and routed in a compatible system
  • Confirmation and activity are logged
  • Unclear cases are sent to a person to review

Fit filter

A good fit starts with a repeatable email workflow.

We check whether the process, email volume, and current systems support a reliable automation before recommending implementation.

Good fit

  • Your team handles repeated support emails that need tickets
  • You can share the current ticket fields and routing rules
  • Your email and ticketing systems offer a feasible integration path
  • You want human review for ambiguous or sensitive cases
  • You are based in India, Ireland, the UK, Australia, or another market

Not a fit

  • You expect automation to work without access or feasibility checks
  • You are only exploring AI with no business workflow
  • You cannot provide tool access or sample data
  • Your process changes every day and has no repeatable pattern
  • You want full custom software at automation pricing

Payment terms

  • New workflows or major changes are quoted separately
