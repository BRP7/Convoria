What it does

Bring two billing views into one review.

The toolkit is designed to make a reconciliation review easier to work through, not to replace the checks and judgement required to resolve a billing issue.

It helps you

  • Compare Microsoft/CSP invoice records against PSA billing records.
  • Surface potential unbilled, over-billed and inconsistent licence lines.
  • Highlight exceptions that need a person to verify source data and commercial context.
  • Keep a more structured record of a reconciliation review.

It does not do

  • Confirm that a discrepancy is an invoice error or recoverable revenue.
  • Automatically change records in Microsoft, a distributor portal or a PSA.
  • Replace internal approvals, contract checks or customer communication.
  • Guarantee compatibility until your export format has been reviewed.

How it works

Designed for a deliberate monthly or quarterly check.

01

Export

Export the relevant Microsoft/CSP invoice or licence data and the corresponding PSA billing data for two billing periods.

02

Map

Review the columns, customer identifiers and product names so that the two sources can be compared meaningfully.

03

Review

Use the output to investigate records that need a closer look, with a human checking the underlying detail.

04

Resolve

Record the outcome in your own process and make any necessary changes only after your usual checks and approvals.

No. A flag only identifies a potential difference between two data sources. It needs manual verification against source records, dates, agreements and your billing rules.
