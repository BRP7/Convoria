What it does

Two billing views, one list of records to check.

The review is a structured check that points you to the records worth a closer look. It does not replace the checks and judgement needed to resolve a billing issue.

It gives you

  • A comparison of Microsoft or distributor invoice quantities against what you bill each customer.
  • A list of possible unbilled, over-billed and inconsistent licence lines.
  • An exceptions list for names that did not match, duplicates, credits and missing periods.
  • A walkthrough call to go through what to verify first.

It does not do

  • Confirm that a difference is an invoice error or recoverable revenue.
  • Change anything in Microsoft, a distributor portal or your billing system.
  • Replace your internal approvals, contract checks or customer communication.
  • Guarantee any recovery.

How it works

Four steps, with a person checking at the end.

01

You send two exports

The Microsoft or distributor invoices and your customer billing export for the last two billing periods. Customer-level licence counts only.

02

We match

Customer and product names are mapped so equivalent records can be compared. Anything that cannot be mapped is listed, never dropped.

03

We report

Differences are separated into those in both months, those in one month only, and over-billing. You receive a findings list and an exceptions list.

04

You verify

We walk through the list on a call. Any change is made only after your usual checks and approvals.

What is included

The review.

  • Comparison of your last two billing periods.
  • A findings list, with the items to verify first.
  • An exceptions list for unmapped names, duplicates, credits and missing periods.
  • A walkthrough call.
Limitations

Important before you start.

  • Every billing system, distributor export and product catalogue can differ.
  • Your data may need column or product mapping before a useful comparison is possible.
  • Use the results as an investigation list, not an automatic billing decision.
  • The method has been built and tested on fictional data. A first real export may need extra mapping work.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost?

The review is £495 for up to 25 customers or £795 for 26 to 60, with half paid to start and half on delivery. Larger businesses are quoted after we see the export formats.

What do I receive?

A findings list with the items to verify first, an exceptions list, and a walkthrough call, covering your last two billing periods.

Has it been tested on real MSP data?

Not yet. The method has been built and tested on fictional data. We expect some real exports to need mapping work, and we agree scope with you before starting.

Will this work with my billing system or distributor?

It may work where your export contains enough customer, product, period, quantity and billing information. Export formats vary, so we review them before agreeing scope.

Does a flagged record mean I have lost revenue?

No. A flag only identifies a possible difference between two data sources. It needs manual verification against source records, dates, agreements and your billing rules.

What data do you need, and how is it protected?

Customer-level licence counts only, with no user names, and customer names can be replaced with codes. The transfer method, who can access the files and when they are deleted are agreed in writing before any files are shared.

Is this software?

No. It is a done-for-you service. It supports a structured review and does not replace your billing system.

How do I start?

Email us with the systems and export formats involved. We reply within one working day, and scope and terms are agreed in writing before any work starts.
