How it works

  1. Export two filesYour Microsoft/CSP invoice and your PSA billing lines, for two billing periods.
  2. Paste them inMap your customer names and licence names once. The template flags anything it cannot match.
  3. Read the summarySee recurring gaps, one-period gaps, exceptions and what to verify first.

What you get

  • The working spreadsheet, with a sample already filled in.
  • A step-by-step guide to matching real exports.
  • An exceptions list: unmapped names, duplicates, credits, missing periods.
  • A verification log, so only confirmed items count as recoverable.

What it cannot tell you

A gap is a lead, not lost revenue. Contracts, discounts, bundles, proration and billing policy can all explain a difference.

Only your contracts and customer invoices can confirm what is billable.

Questions

Which PSAs and distributors does it support?

It works from exports, so any PSA or distributor that can export customer, product, period, quantity and price should work after mapping. It is early access, and some formats will need extra mapping.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. The template needs two exports. We never ask for logins.

Will it find lost revenue?

It finds differences between two files. Whether they are lost revenue depends on your contracts, which is why the summary separates estimates from confirmed amounts.
